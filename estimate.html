1. Core Boards Explained

  • Smart Hybrid (Included): Combines high-density Action Tesa HDHMR / BWR plywood in dry zones with IS:710 BWP Marine Ply in wet sink cabinets.
  • Merino Marine Board: Uniform high-density moisture-resistant structural board across the entire kitchen or wardrobe.
  • IS:710 BWP Marine Ply: Full boiling-waterproof hardwood core engineered for maximum multi-decade durability.

2. Factory Machine vs. Carpenter

  • Hot-Melt PVC Edge-Banding: Industrial 200°C PUR/EVA bonding seals all 4 board edges—steam and water cannot seep in near sinks or hobs.
  • Precision Panel Saw & CNC: Exact millimeter squareness with concealed minifix/dowel joinery and zero exposed screw heads.
  • Clean 3-Day Installation: Manufactured in-house; assembled quietly at your home without weeks of dust.

3. What Is Included in Base Rate

  • Modular Kitchen Base: Soft-close hinges on all shutters, 5 soft-close tandem drawers, 1 Bottle Pull-Out (BPO), and 1 cutlery tray.
  • Modular Wardrobe Base: Soft-close hinges, 2 smooth-glide drawers, 1 heavy-duty hanging rod, and adjustable internal shelves.
  • Transparent Area Math: The 2 ft empty wall gap between kitchen counter and wall cabinets is never billed.
